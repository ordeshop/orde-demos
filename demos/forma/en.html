What is running here

World
A 3D scene in Three.js. The camera follows a curve through chrome, glass and fourteen rings, driven only by scrolling.
Material
Liquid chrome whose surface a noise field reshapes in every frame. Glass with real refraction. The reflections come from a studio that is computed as well.
Particles
32,000 points that arrange themselves into type, a sphere, a knot, a wave and a swirl, and move out of your mouse’s way.
Type
Archivo as a variable font: the width and weight of every letter follow your mouse and your scroll speed.
Images
Four videos run through a liquid shader: ripples under the mouse, a color shift when you scroll fast, a flowing edge as they appear.
Building blocks
Fonts: Archivo, Instrument Serif and Fragment Mono (SIL Open Font License). Three.js and Lenis (MIT). Everything is served from the same server, nothing from third parties.
