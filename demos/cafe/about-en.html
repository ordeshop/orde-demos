About us

Our story

A room for slow mornings.

MØRK started with a simple idea: a cafe where the coffee is made with care and nobody gets rushed out the door.

We roast our own beans in small batches and grind fresh for every cup. The menu is short on purpose — so we can get every detail right. What lands on your plate is seasonal, mostly local, and always made fresh.

The room is bright, quiet, and built from wood. Dogs are welcome, laptops until noon. After that, the tables are for conversation.

Craft

Every cup, on purpose.

Every morning our baristas taste the batch, dial in the grinder, and make sure the espresso is right before the first guest walks in. Small rituals you can taste.
