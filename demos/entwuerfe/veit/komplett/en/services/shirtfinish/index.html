VEIT RENTAL OFFER PAY PER PIECE (PPP)

When purchasing a new shirt finisher or a shirt press, one question always arises for companies: buy or lease? VEIT GmbH offers its customers a new variant: PAY PER PIECE

No matter whether the textile care company wants to start with the shirt business and needs the complete workstation or if the shirt finisher only needs to be replaced, with VEIT the customer only pays for the number of shirts that he has actually finished.

What is exactly happening in this new billing system?

The number of units is recorded daily and, on the basis of the meter reading, the units are transmitted via the mobile phone network to VEIT GmbH as also, when desired, to an email address of the companies’ choice

This meter reading is now used to calculate the daily rent of the shirt finisher or shirt press on a monthly basis.

The VEIT customer therefore only pays per shirt produced.

The cent amount per shirt depends on the equipment of the finisher or press and whether other machines and equipment (e.g. collar & cuff press, ironing table, steam generator) are included in the PPP rental model.

PPP – YOUR ADVANTAGES:

  • Pay only according to use
    If only a few shirts are handed in at the businesses in a low-turnover month, the rent in that month is also correspondingly less.
  • No capital commitment
    Since the PPP billing system is not a hire-purchase or leasing arrangement, neither capital is tied up nor does it affect the credit line of the textile care businesses. This gives the owners of the textile care businesses room to manoeuvre.
  • Planning security
    The cent amount per shirt is set for the next 5 years when the rental contract is signed. This means planning security for the businesses. The owner already knows his expenses for the next few years.
  • Maintenance and service included
    During the entire rental period, all maintenance and any repair work is included in the rental per shirt. This applies for the entire rental period. The VEIT remote access for remote diagnosis and status queries is included. This means that fault diagnoses, service and updates can be carried out immediately online.
