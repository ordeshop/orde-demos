Welcome to the VEIT in-house exhibition ISA

Thank you for your visit in Landsberg am Lech, Germany
9 to 11 September 2025

The industry meeting place for technical applications

Interior, Furniture, Aircraft, Railway, Mobility, Automotive and many more

VEIT opened its doors in Landsberg am Lech from Tuesday 9 September to Thursday 11 September 2025 and brought together international companies from a wide range of industries such as Interior, Furniture, Aircraft, Railway, Mobility and Automotive with experienced industry partners (manufacturers and suppliers) at the in-house exhibition ISA 2025. The VEIT in-house exhibition ISA took place parallel to the IAA Mobility in Munich.

VEIT PRODUCT HIGHLIGHTS

Also experience the wide range of VEIT products, the number 1 in laminating and finishing technology.

Laminating Machine LM

With its new LM laminating machine, VEIT has the ideal solution for gentle laminating of flexible materials, such as leather, textiles, foils etc. with foam, felt, non-woven or knit materials. In addition to cuts, LM can be equipped with a roll-off/roll-on device for yard goods.

Winding and rewinding device WR16

The winding and rewinding device is the perfect addition to your VEIT laminating or fusing machine. A fully automatic process of unwinding individual rolls, then rewinding into a single roll after the laminating or fusing process.

Vaporization system - Interior Finisher IF60

The VEIT IF60 Interior Finisher is suitable for all interior parts. It provides a consistent, excellent quality finish for all types of fabrics and leather-covered surfaces.
