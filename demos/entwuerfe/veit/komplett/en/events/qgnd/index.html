QUALITY GARMENT NETWORK DAYS 2020 - New date will follow

Exit restrictions, closures, the current situation affects us all, our society, our economy.

In view of the dynamic development around SARS-CoV2/ Covid-19 we want to act responsibly and postpone
the seventh QUALITY GARMENT NETWORK DAYS (15-17 October 2020) until further notice.

The seventh QUALITY GARMENT NETWORK DAYS will take place at a later date.

As soon as the situation returns to normal, we will publish the the new date as soon as possible.

This exceptional situation is new for all of us. Thank you for your understanding.
Cohesion and a solidary cooperation are our top priority.

Do not miss the garment industry`s quality event of the year and join us.

The event is conceived for participants to benefit in three different ways:

  • Professional training by experts
  • Immediate implementation in workshops
  • Extension of your personal network of specialist contacts.

We are looking forward to seeing you!
