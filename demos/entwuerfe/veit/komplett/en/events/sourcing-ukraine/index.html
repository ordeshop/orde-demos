Sourcing Ukraine

VEIT Networking Days 2020 have been cancelled - New date ​will follow

In view of the dynamic development around SARS-CoV-2 (“corona virus”) and the increasing risk of infection in Germany, we want to act responsibly and therefore cancel the VEIT Networking Days.

The VEIT Networking Days will nevertheless take place at a later date. We will publish the new date as soon as possible.

Thank you for your understanding.

From 06 to 07 May 2020 the VEIT Networking Days – Sourcing Ukraine will take place for the first time.

Once a year VEIT opens the door in Landsberg and brings German brands together with international producers. They get the chance to present themselves and their country with all regularities. Networking events offer the chance to get to know new business partners.

We bring you together.

Program

Your benefits

  • Get to know the Ukraine as a lucrative European production location
  • Make valuable contacts
  • Benefit from logistics knowledge about freight forwarding, customs duties, import and export times and regulations

Highlights

  • 3 expert presentations
  • Producer power speeches, exclusive dating time with producers
  • Conference catering
  • Evening event/Networking
  • VEIT Workshop bookable as a free additional option

Target group

  • Managing Directors
  • Production Managers
  • Sourcing Managers
  • Product Managers
  • Quality Managers
