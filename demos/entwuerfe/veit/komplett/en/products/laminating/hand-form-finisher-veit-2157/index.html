Product: hand/form finisher for fusing interior parts

For manual finishing

Hand- /Form Finisher 2157

Weight: 620 g / 1.37 lbs
Ironing surface: 125 x 85 mm / 4.92 x .3.35 inch
100 – 240 V, 600 W, 50 – 60 Hz

For manual finishing/fusing of fabric or leather- covered interior parts and for removing creases, shine and marks on interior surfaces.
Our high-quality VEIT form finisher ensures a wrinkle-free and pressure-free interior.

Advantages:

  • The teflon frame sole attachment is used for smoothing or improving the shape of uneven surfaces
  • Whereas the steam brush attachment gives an excellent appearance to leather, velours or delicate fabrics
  • Easy handling & fast, effective work
