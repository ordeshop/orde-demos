TF 57 Tunnel Finisher

Request a quote Find a contact

The ideal solution for refinishing garments and unrivaled finishing results

The VEIT TF 57 Tunnel Finisher is the highly automated solution for finishing textiles in high numbers. It is ideally suited for any textile on the market, including challenging natural fibres. The system performs highly at, among others, textile logistics and refinishing companies, mail order companies as well as for garment manufacturers and medical companies. An hourly performance of about 2500 pieces maximizes your output. The VEIT TF 57 Tunnel Finisher’s modular system allows a wide variety of individual applications.

Advantages:

  • Safe transport of the goods with the conveyor and the VEIT hook system
  • No condensate at conveyor: Suction at the entry prevents the formation of drops or stains on the garment
  • Clearly separated processes: With roller unit between preconditioning and finish
  • Consistent finishing quality for any material thanks to the adjustable level of humidity in the spraying steam
  • High blowing power: The new generation of blowers is highly effi cient and energy-saving, with minimum noise emission
  • Optimized finishing result: the extended air module increases performance
  • Maintenance-free chain tensioning system: The pneumatic transport hook system is nearly free from wear and tear
  • Impressive steam doll effect: The high-volume exit module adds extra volume to the garment
  • Adjustable separation between steam and air module avoids marks on the goods during the finishing process
  • Lower part of steam unit can be switched off when finishing basic garments. Yet another energy-saving feature
  • Finishing process visualized: The modern touch display can be flexibly positioned

The benefits in energy efficiency:

  • Efficient blower generation with high performance and reduced energy
  • Flow-optimised airflow to minimize losses
  • Height-adjustable exit module for less heat loss
  • Insulation helps to save energy

More

Moisture Module

With its Moisture module, the VEIT TF 57 Tunnel Finisher achieves unrivaled finishing results, even for difficult-to-process natural fibres.

Advantages:

  • Significantly improved finishing results for natural fibres due to efficient preconditioning of natural fibres with water spraying technology
  • Reduced steam consumption in the steam module resulting in less energy consumption thanks to the integrated Moisture module
  • Higher efficiency

Steam Module

In the Steam Module the garment is treated with saturated steam, making the fibre swell and formable. The circulating air module with its elaborate air flow straightens and stretches the garment, which is simultaneously dried by hot air.

Advantages:

  • Adjustable steam quality: Customised humidity of steam spray for reliable finishing performance
  • No unwelcome streaks on the garment thanks to consistent steam distribution
  • Avoids garment damage from hot surfaces
  • Easy cleaning helps to keep the machine in a good state

Air Module

Advantages:

  • Consistent, energy-efficient drying and smoothing, even with high pass-through speeds
  • Reduced flow losses, optimized air circulation, perfect finishing
  • Easy neutralisation of odours during the drying cycle

Exit Module

Advantages:

  • Stunning steam doll effect with perfect voluminous finishing touch
  • Height of exit module is automatically adjustable
