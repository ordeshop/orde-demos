Modular Tunnel Finisher System for Maximum Flexibility

The VEIT TF 57 Tunnel Finisher is based on a modular system to allow a wide variety of individual solutions. Requirements or applications have changed? No problem at all. Upgrading or extending a tunnel is easy. Individual tunnel solutions can be implemented according to application, capacity and type of product that needs to be finished. For whatever combination is chosen, there is one common principle: The longer the tunnel, the faster the throughput of goods. VEIT experts are happy to individually discuss the most economical solution for any requirements.
