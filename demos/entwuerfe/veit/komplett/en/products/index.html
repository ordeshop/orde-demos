Products

VEIT textile care products

We at VEIT are your professional manufacturer of high-quality products in the clothing, textile care and interior sectors. Our range includes product groups such as: Industrial irons, ironing boards, steam generators, ironing presses, finishing devices, trousers toppers, tunnel finishers, fusing machines, laminating, stain removal, laying and folding tables, vacuum and compressed air, steam systems and other devices for textile care. We are particularly proud that the products of our family company are used in over 100 countries: a sure sign of the wide selection and high quality of the VEIT Group products.

Our products are used here

Our customers include well-known companies from international and global competition. Starting with well-known clothing manufacturers and textile conditioners to textile care companies, dry cleaners and laundries. Our machines maintain and clean a wide variety of textiles from the clothing industry, such as jackets, blazers, coats, pants, skirts, dresses, shirts, blouses, jackets and knitwear. They remove stains, clean, finish and iron the textiles using the latest technology without damaging them. Also in the automotive industry, for professional interior care, our VEIT group products convince in regular use.
