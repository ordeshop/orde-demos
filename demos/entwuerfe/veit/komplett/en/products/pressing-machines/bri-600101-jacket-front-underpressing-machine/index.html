BRI 600 Jacket Front Underpressing Machine

Request a quote Find a contact

Jacket front underpressing machine (concave pressing bucks)

Advantages:

  • Patented carriage system for left and right front parts / saves space
  • Vertical head buck closing: Even distribution of pressure onto the entire pressing surface and prevents displacement of the garment on the machine: Increases quality
  • User-friendly 5.7“ colour touch display
  • Separate program selection switch: Allows quick change of programs
  • Steam edge suction: Avoids steam marks
  • Fork for dart peaks: Dart ends can be ironed more easily (optional)
  • Electromechanical distance control (optional)
  • Concave pressing bucks: Ideal for carrying out various operations:
    • 1. Opening of side seams and chest darts by iron
    • 2. Insertion of plack piece incl. adhesive tapes
    • 3. Form pressing
