SG 67 Steam generator with steam applicator

Request a quote Find a contact

Cleaning and disinfection without chemical additives

The VEIT steam generator with steam applicator is an indispensable aid. It is designed for a very wide range of applications due to its high-quality workmanship. The device is used to clean residues during the production of dentures, bridges and inlays, etc. It is also used for disinfection (e.g. for fittings, handles, laboratory items, etc.) to combat viruses and germs without the use of chemicals.

This small electro steam generator/boiler SG 67 is designed for continuous operation in the following industrial applications garment and textiles industry, automotive industry, chemical industry, production and dental laboratories, hospitals and pharmacies, food and beverage sector, packaging sector, iron and steel industry.

In addition to the modern product design, it shines through its reliable and easy handling. The steam applicator is available separately for connection to the SG 67 and the SG 66MF.

Advantages:

  • 1 device, 2 solutions: Cleaning and disinfection
  • Small size
  • Easy handling
  • Can also be operated without direct water connection
  • Long service life
  • Incl. safety cleaning tank

Which machine is right for you?

That depends on your garments, volumes and floor space. VEIT customer advisors and local sales partners will work it out with you.
