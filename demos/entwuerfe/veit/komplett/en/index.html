We bring fabric into shape.

VEIT builds machines and systems for ironing, fusing and finishing textiles. A family business since 1956, based in Landsberg am Lech, Germany, with products in use in more than 100 countries.

What do you need a machine for?

Three industries, one craft: smoothing, shaping and bonding fabric, from a single ironing station to a complete system with conveyor technology.

  • Garment industry

    Jackets, blazers and coats, trousers, skirts, dresses, shirts and blouses, knitwear, textile reconditioning

  • Textile care

  • Interior
