70 years of VEIT: Family-run business celebrates anniversary with staff, families and international partners

29. September 2026

Landsberg am Lech, July 2026 – On 26 June 2026, the VEIT Group celebrated its 70th anniversary at its headquarters in Landsberg am Lech. Employees and their families, as well as long-standing sales partners from various countries, came together to look back on the family-run business’s development since 1956 and to discuss the future of textile processing.

The anniversary celebrations focused on the people who have shaped and supported VEIT over the years. The day provided an opportunity for personal encounters between staff from different departments, former colleagues and international sales partners. At the same time, staff families gained an insight into the company, its products and its international operations.

During factory tours, guests were able to experience VEIT’s production processes and technological developments at first hand. For the youngest visitors, there was a varied family programme featuring face painting and games, ensuring that they too could actively enjoy the day.

The anniversary celebrations highlighted what has driven the company since 1956: technical curiosity, long-term partnerships and the dedication of its staff. A particular highlight of the celebrations was the personal address given by Günter Veit (VEIT Group President), who traced the company’s history from its beginnings to the present day and emphasised the importance of solidarity, continuity and a spirit of innovation for the success of the family-run business.

“VEIT’s 70th anniversary symbolises the courage to continually break new technical ground – but above all, it honours the people who have built the company with their knowledge, passion and reliability,” said Gunter Veit. “This anniversary is therefore, first and foremost, a thank you to our employees and their families, as well as to our customers and partners, some of whom have been with us for many decades.”

Technological developments from Landsberg am Lech

The company’s history began in 1956 when it was founded by Reinhardt Veit. Even in its early years, VEIT developed new technical solutions for industrial ironing and the processing of clothing.

Among the most significant developments were the cold-ironing technology developed by Reinhardt Veit and the subsequent introduction of suction and blowing technology. These processes enabled gentler, more uniform and more productive processing of garments and contributed significantly to the further industrialisation of clothing production.

Over the course of seven decades, the company – which initially specialised in ironing technology – has grown into an internationally active group of companies. Today, VEIT develops and manufactures machinery and equipment for ironing, pressing, fusing, laminating and finishing garments and technical textiles.

The solutions range from individual workstations and machines to automated systems and complete production lines. They are used, amongst other things, in the clothing industry, in laundries and textile care facilities, in logistics and returns centres, and in the processing of technical textiles. VEIT systems are currently in use by customers in more than 100 countries.

A family-run business with an international focus

Despite its global operations, VEIT remains closely linked to its home town of Landsberg am Lech to this day. As a family-run business, the group places great emphasis on long-term relationships, its own technological expertise and close collaboration with customers, employees and sales partners.

“Our history shows that long-term success does not stem from individual products alone,” said Christopher Veit (VEIT Group Vice President). “What is crucial is a willingness to embrace change, a deep understanding of our customers’ applications and the trust that grows between people over many years.”

A look at the next generation of textile processing

The anniversary was not just an occasion for looking back. Issues such as automation, energy efficiency, digitalisation and the growing shortage of skilled workers are changing the demands placed on the textile and clothing industry. At the same time, new areas of application for textile materials are emerging, for example in the automotive industry, in technical textiles and in automated fashion logistics.

VEIT intends to continue supporting this development with practical technologies. The aim is to help customers combine quality, productivity and resource efficiency.

The anniversary celebrations highlighted what has driven the company since 1956: technical curiosity, long-term partnerships and the commitment of its staff.
