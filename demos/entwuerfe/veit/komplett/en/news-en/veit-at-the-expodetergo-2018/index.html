VEIT at the EXPOdetergo 2018

27. November 2018

Landsberg am Lech, Germany, 04 October 2018. The leading manufacturer of ironing technology is displaying its products to trade professionals at the EXPOdetergo trade fair in the Italian city of Milan, which takes place every four years. From 19 to 22 October 2018, there will be a lot to explore on the more than 100-square-metre area in Hall 3, booth M22.

The market leader, based in Landsberg am Lech, Germany, is going to present its wide range of products on a completely redesigned booth at the EXPOdetergo.

Fast, high-quality, and engergy-efficient

processing to textiles – that is what the VEIT machines displayed at the EXPOdetergo excel in, in the year 2018 more than ever before.

Four shirt systems on display at the EXPOdetergo in Milan

VEIT´s emphasis at this year´s trade fair is on shirt processing. Four different energy-efficient systems will be shown in real-life situations during the exhibition.

There is not only the Shirt Finisher with its heat recovery system and intelligent process shortening function thanks to VEIT´s moisture control sensors. Visitors can also find out about the “Pay Per Piece” settlement scheme. Instead of having to purchase the machine, customers will also have the option to pay only for how much they actually use it. For quantities of 1,800 or more shirts processed per month, servicing and maintenance of the machine is included for costs starting at just 16 cent per shirt.

Another highlight is the VEIT 8900 Shirt Press, which is also available with the new “Pay Per Piece” settlement scheme.

“LOVEIT or LOVE IT”:

With this slogan, all the machines for professional textile care will be presented at the EXPOdetergo live and under operating conditions with steam. Visitors can thus experience the variety and high quality of the machines directly at the booth – all of which are made in Germany, manufactured at the production facilities in Landsberg am Lech.

“For us, it goes without saying that customer benefit is our top priority” says Jürgen Pfattheicher, Head of Sales at VEIT. “Customers can see that for themselves in a real-life experience at the EXPOdetergo 2018.”

The VEIT Group

For over 60 years, VEIT Group´s products for shaping and finishing garments and fabrics have been the epitome of outstanding quality and highest productivity. This is why garment manufactures as well as textile care companies, garment refinishers and customers from the automotive industry in more than 100 countries rely on VEIT technology. With numerous subsidiaries and an excellent distributor network around the world, VEIT Group continues to grow globally.

The product portfolio covers everything from traditional ironing tables with hand irons and steam generators, as well as presses and finishers, and also includes blower toppers and tunnel finishers.
