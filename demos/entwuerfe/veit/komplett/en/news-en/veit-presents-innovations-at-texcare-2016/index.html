VEIT Presents Innovations at TEXCARE 2016

10. April 2018

Landsberg am Lech, Germany, 18 April 2016. The market leader for ironing technology is displaying its products to trade professionals at the TEXCARE trade fair in Frankfurt am Main, which takes place every four years. From 11 to 15 June 2016, there will be a lot to explore on the 250-square metre-area in Hall 9, booths C30 and D40.

This year, VEIT is celebrating its 60th anniversary. With Günter and Christopher Veit being at the helm of the family-run enterprise, it is the second and third generation who are jointly steering the company. To honour the company’s history and to present its wide range of products, the market leader from Landsberg in Southern Germany has yet again expanded its booth surface and is promising a new stand design at the TEXCARE.

Displaying machines for fast, high-quality, and energy-efficient processing of textiles at the TEXCARE is very much in the focus of VEIT, now more than ever before. Traditionally, the fair in Frankfurt sees new standards being set for the industry for the years to come.

VEIT is going to exhibit several energy-efficient systems for processing shirts: There is not only the Shirt Finisher with its heat recovery system and intelligent process time shortening function thanks to VEIT’s moisture control sensors. Visitors can also find out about the new „Pay Per Piece“ system. Instead of buying the machine, customers pay only for how much they actually use it. If, say, 1,400 to 2,000 shirts are processed per month, servicing and maintenance of the machine will be included for just 19 cents per shirt.

At the TEXCARE trade fair, the new „Pay Per Piece“ rental system will also be available for other items.

„Easy for you“: With this slogan, machines for professional textile care will be presented at the TEXCARE live and under operating conditions with steam. Visitors can thus see directly at the booth the variety and high quality of the machines, which are all made in Germany – manufactured at the production facilities in Landsberg
„For us, it goes without saying that customer benefit is our top priority“ says Jürgen Pfattheicher, Sales Director at VEIT. „Customers can see live at the TEXCARE 2016“.
The product portfolio covers everything from traditional ironing tables with hand irons and steam generators, as well as presses, finishers and also includes blower toppers and tunnel finishers.

The VEIT Group

For over 60 years,VEIT Group’s products for finishing garments and fabrics have been the epitome of outstanding quality and highest productivity. This is why garment manufacturers as well as garment refinishers, logistics companies and customers from the automotive industry in more than 100 countries rely on VEIT technology. With numerous subsidiaries and an excellent distributor network around the world, VEIT Group continues to grow globally.
