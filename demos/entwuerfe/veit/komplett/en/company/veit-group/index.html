VEIT Group

Why you can put your trust in the VEIT Group

For 60 years, the requirements and the problems of our customers world-wide have been our most important challenge. For decades, world-famous brands in the German and the international garment industry have had confidence in the innovative, high-quality products and services of the VEIT Group.

Our experts consult with you as a partner to assist you in finding the best solution for your project no matter if you are planning to use individual machines or if you need a complete production line.

Prompt delivery and professional installation of your machines and plants goes hand in hand with premium training with which we enable your staff to achieve the highest possible productivity.

Our service technicians worldwide are available around the clock so that your production works with as little disruption as possible.

We look forward to dealing with you personally!
