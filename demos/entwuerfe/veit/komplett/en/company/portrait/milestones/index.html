Milestones

60 Years of VEIT

VEIT GmbH is a medium-sized family company. The story began more than 60 years ago and developed to be the world´s leading manufacture of industrial finishing and ironing technology. More than 60 successful years of corporate history are a reliable basis for us to continue to grow, both in Germany and across the globe.

  • 2014 Christopher Veit was appointed General Manager
  • 2011 VEIT Zhejiang Co. Ltd., China was founded
  • 2009 VEIT Representative Office Bangladesh was founded
  • 2001 Kannegiesser GTT, Vlotho was acquired
  • 2000 VEIT Dominicana, Dominican Republik was founded
  • 1999 VEIT Indo, Indonesia was founded
  • 1999 Shares of Nihon VEIT, Japan, were aquired
  • 1999 VEIT UK, England was founded
  • 1997 VEIT Romania was founded
  • 1995 Ramsons-VEIT, Indien was founded
  • 1995 VEIT Asia Private Limited, Singapore was founded
  • 1994 VEIT Hong Kong Limited was founded
  • 1994 VEIT Representative Office, Vietnam was founded
  • 1994 VEIT Representative Office, Singapore was founded
  • 1994 Shanghai-VEIT was founded
  • 1990 VEIT France, Lille, France was founded
  • 1989 BRISAY GmbH, Aschaffenburg, Germany was acquired
  • 1980 VEIT Inc. Atlanta, USA was founded
  • 1956 The company was founded by Reinhardt Veit
