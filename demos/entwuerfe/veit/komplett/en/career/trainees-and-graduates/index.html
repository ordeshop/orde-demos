Trainees and Graduates

Your vocational training with VEIT

Shaping the future

You are part of the future of your company. We do our very best to provide you with the best possible vocational training. You systematically learn all the know-how and skills you need for the processes in your future profession. You will enjoy your training thanks to the
interesting tasks and the good working conditions.

Our aim is to give you the self-assuredness and the professional basis to start a successful future career.

.

Training and values

Mutual trust is our basis.

We create a climate that promotes trust through

  • Sincerity, honesty and candidness
  • Reliability
  • Responsibility

People are more important to us than objects. We show this through mutual respect, open cooperation, trust and sincerity. We support and care for each other.

Our "AZUBI-Team"

In Germany, vocational trainees are called "Azubis". The ability to work in a team is an important factor for your training and for your future career. Information, communication and competence are the most important indicators of a good team - this is what we want to show you.

You will have the opportunity to show your team spirit, because our "AZUBI-Team" get their own tasks and projects.

Your opportunities

We care a lot about your professional career and your personal success. This is why we offer an extraordinary all-round education with theoretical input and hands-on experience, to the benefit of both yourself and the company.

We promote your talents. You can shape your successful training through your own commitment, your team spirit and by focusing on your goals. Love what you do, and you will succeed.

Our future

As a qualified future employee, you are setting the path for the future of our company and for your own success.

Your vocational training does not lead to a dead end, but to various career options with VEIT.

Customer friendliness

Customers monitor our conduct every day. In the different departments of our company, your colleagues and superiors will be "your customers'" - representing the customers in field.

Trainers and managers make sure that each customer gets optimal care. So each of us, in their own role and in their own way, is responsible for their "customers" satisfaction.

Competence

Competence for us means your personal development.

Your vocational training will give you the capacity to take responsible actions and decisions in your future job life. That, for us, is competence, too.
