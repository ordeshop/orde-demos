03 / 06 · Path morph · Shape chain

One shape becomes the next.

Five key poses: circle, square, triangle, star, flower. The browser computes everything in between, 240 points in every frame. The blue lines are the in-betweens that just went by.

04 / 06 · Trim Paths · Line

A line that draws itself.

The start and the end of a line run on different curves, so it chases itself along a path of 900 points. The mouse detunes the oscillation.

05 / 06 · Repeater · Copies

Built once, turned a hundred times.

A single stroke, copied by fixed rules. At the golden angle of 137.5°, the copies line up like the seeds of a sunflower. The mouse twists every copy.

06 / 06 · Stagger · Grid

Not all at once.

Each cell starts a little later than its neighbor. The offset turns one movement into a wave: from the left, from the center, from the corner, at random. Click the grid and it answers.
